How the challenge works

Each day has one trick, a goal, three steps, a “done when” line and a way to make it harder. The weeks build on each other: foundations first, then body awareness, clever paws and a showtime week, with two bonus days to finish.

  • Two or three sessions of up to five minutes beat one long session. Stop while your collie still wants more.
  • Use small treats from the daily food portion, and practise on a non-slip floor or rug.
  • Skip any position your dog does not enjoy and come back to it another day. The board does not mind the order.
  • Your progress and the name you type are saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent to us, and there is no account.

Tricks give a working mind a daily job. For the bigger picture, read our guides on the Border Collie teenage phase and on owner exercise routines and rest days.
